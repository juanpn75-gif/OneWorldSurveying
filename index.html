About One World Surveying

Surveying knowledge, workflow development and geospatial technology.

oneworldsurveying.com

One World Surveying is a surveying and geospatial technology resource created by Juan A. Pichardo, PLS. The site brings together practical tools and workflows for land surveying, GNSS control, LiDAR and point-cloud work, mapping, coordinate systems, CAD, and field-to-office production.
